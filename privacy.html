Legal Terms & Privacy Policy

Transparency, minimal data retention, and zero voice audio storage are our core engineering principles. Review our terms of service, acceptable use rules, and data handling practices below.

Table of Contents

Zero Audio Storage

VoiceClaw never listens to, records, or transmits voice packets. All voice calls run directly over Discord's native infrastructure.

01

Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how VoiceClaw ("we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, and safeguards information when you invite our Discord application to your server or interact with its automated voice channels and slash commands.

By using VoiceClaw, you acknowledge and agree to the data collection and usage practices described in this document. We respect your privacy and comply strictly with applicable data protection laws, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

02

Information We Collect & Process

To provision dynamic voice channels and enforce channel owner permissions, VoiceClaw processes a strictly minimal set of non-sensitive metadata:

Data Point Purpose Storage Type
User Discord Snowflake ID Identifying channel owner, applying permission overwrites, trusted/blocked user lists, and rate limiting. Stored in Database
Server (Guild) ID Associating server hubs, custom bitrates, and category structures to the guild. Stored in Database
Voice Channel IDs Managing temporary room lifecycles and executing instant deletion when the room is empty. Ephemeral (Until Empty)
Custom Room Settings Remembering custom channel names, user limits, and locked states configured by the host. Stored in Database
Voice Audio Data NEVER COLLECTED. VoiceClaw has zero microphone or audio streaming capabilities. Never Stored
Message Content / DMs NEVER STORED. Slash command and button interaction payloads are ephemeral. Never Stored
Zero Commercialization Guarantee: VoiceClaw never sells, rents, monetizes, or distributes user data or server identifiers to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
03

Voice Audio & Communication Privacy

VoiceClaw operates strictly as a channel orchestrator. We want to be unequivocally clear regarding your conversations:

  • The bot does not join voice channels as an audio listener or speaker.
  • The bot does not process, route, stream, transcribe, or capture voice packets.
  • All voice communication occurs entirely over Discord's encrypted infrastructure, governed exclusively by Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
04

Data Retention & Automatic Purging

We enforce strict data minimization and automated record purging:

  • Temporary Channel Records: The moment the last user leaves a temporary voice channel, the Discord channel is deleted and its database record in voiceChannel is purged within 3 seconds.
  • Server Configurations: Guild settings persist only while VoiceClaw resides in the guild. When removed, all associated hub configs are purged.
  • User Settings: Custom user preferences persist until manually reset by the user or upon 180 consecutive days of global inactivity.
05

Your Rights & Data Removal (GDPR / CCPA)

Under international privacy regulations, you have full authority over your data:

  1. Right of Access: You may request a summary of any stored preferences associated with your Discord user ID.
  2. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the immediate deletion of all records associated with your account at any time.
  3. Right to Rectification: You can update or override your custom settings anytime via the Discord UI Control Panel.

To request full data erasure, click the "Request Data Deletion" button in the navigation bar or contact our team in the official support server.

Acceptable Use & Prohibited Conduct

When using VoiceClaw, you agree not to engage in any abusive or disruptive conduct, including:

  • Abuse & Spamming: Rapidly triggering join-to-create channels with automated scripts or macros in an attempt to flood server audit logs, disrupt services, or exceed Discord API rate limits.
  • Inappropriate Content: Setting temporary voice room names containing hate speech, harassment, explicit content, or material violating server guidelines.
  • Exploitation: Attempting to reverse engineer, disrupt, inject malicious payloads, or circumvent bot cooldown and security constraints.
  • Discord TOS Breaches: Utilizing VoiceClaw voice rooms to facilitate illegal activities or conduct prohibited by Discord.
Violating acceptable use standards will result in server blacklisting, bot command timeouts, or formal reporting to Discord Trust & Safety.

  • Ensuring server rules and community standards are upheld across all dynamic rooms.
04

Service Availability & Modifications

VoiceClaw is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis. While we target 99.9% uptime with high-performance async backends, operation may be briefly paused for maintenance, database index optimization, or upstream Discord Gateway incidents.

We reserve the right to modify, upgrade, or refine bot features at our discretion to enhance security, performance, and user experience.

01

Security Architecture & Storage

All database transactions are handled through high-performance encrypted connections (TLS/SSL). We enforce strict security protocols:

  • No Plaintext Credentials: Bot tokens, session cookies, and API keys are stored strictly in protected environment secrets.
  • Database Isolation: Data stores are isolated behind private VPC firewalls with automated rate limiting to prevent DDoS and slowloris attempts.
  • No Sensitive PII: VoiceClaw never handles, requests, or stores email addresses, phone numbers, real names, or payment card details.
02

Discord OAuth2 & Gateway Permissions

When inviting VoiceClaw, the bot requests specific Discord OAuth2 permissions. Here is the operational necessity for each:

  • Manage Channels: Required to dynamically spawn temporary voice channels under designated categories and delete them upon emptiness.
  • Move Members: Automatically moves the connected user from the "Join to Create" channel directly into their private spawned channel.
  • Manage Permissions: Allows channel owners to lock, unlock, hide, trust, and block members by adjusting channel overwrites.
  • Send Messages & Embeds: Renders interactive Discord Components V2 control interfaces and configuration dashboards.
